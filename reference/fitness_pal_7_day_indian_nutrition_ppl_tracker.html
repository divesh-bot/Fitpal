Core Nutrition Rules

Important
  • Whole eggs only: No liquid or separated egg whites. If more protein is needed, increase low-fat paneer, Greek yogurt, soya chunks, or dal. Count the full egg.
  • No tofu: Strictly excluded from all meals.
  • Tuesday strictly vegetarian: No eggs or chicken on Tuesdays.
  • Measure oil & coffee additions: Track cooking oil (typically 3–5g/meal) and coffee toned milk (100 ml) + sugar (5 g).
  • Verify pack labels: Greek yogurt, paneer, soya, and milk vary by brand. Edit any ingredient directly in the Today tab.

Supplements & Protein Milk

Creatine Monohydrate 5 g daily

Timing is flexible; take on rest days too. No loading phase needed. Scale weight may temporarily rise due to water stored in muscle.

Country Delight Protein Milk 450 ml • 293 kcal • 25g P

Have after morning workout. Always count its 293 kcal in your daily total.

BCAA (Intra-workout) Optional

Offers little extra benefit if total daily protein (~160g) is met with complete protein sources. Consider whether you need to continue.

Review & Accuracy Protocol

  • 1. Weigh in every morning after using the washroom and before food or drink. Compare 7-day averages, not single readings.
  • 2. Measure waist once weekly under similar conditions and record gym performance.
  • 3. Review after 2–3 weeks of consistent tracking before adjusting calories. Check cooking oil, portions, labels, and adherence first.
  • 4. Historical personal-best lifts (170 / 200 / 100 kg) are not current training targets. Rebuild technique and work capacity gradually.

General nutrition guidance, not a medical diet. Discuss persistent unusual salt cravings with dizziness or weakness with a clinician.
